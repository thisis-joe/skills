# Isolated sandbox procedure comparisons

The comparisons do not justify adopting any of the three sandbox proposals.
R5 has an unresolved measurement pair and no fewer confirmed policy repairs in
the four usable pairs. R2 completes all five fresh-context handoffs in both arms,
but misses the required 20% time reduction. R7 retains the same kinds of next-step
recommendations, while its replies are longer. These findings concern the tested
changes; they do not erase the demonstrated value of a shared procedure.

The user authorized documentation and temporary-project experiments only. The
accepted development decisions, active skills, installed skills and inert upstream
sources are unchanged. Task state: [execution plan](../../plans/procedure-refinement.md).

## Comparison and retained evidence

The control is the decision-bearing `622975e` snapshot used by both the
[workflow report v2](../staged-development/report-v2.md) and
[collaboration comparison](../collaboration-value/report.md). This is not a test
of a different release branch. The frozen [protocol](protocol.md) and

| Factor | Isolated intervention | Actual tasks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R5 | A short frontier of agreed, owner-owned and reversible decisions in the clarification opening | Service billing, retention, rankings, interval union and contact consolidation; two undisclosed owner policies per domain |
| R2 | Reinforce updating the existing feature spec and sole plan, linking prior decisions and reporting original/actual/reason | Five purchasing CLI/SQLite projects; writer implements import/list, fresh successor completes summary |
| R7 | Returning-user advice led by one next stage/skill, purpose, result and usable request | Unsettled requirements, ready implementation, unexpected regression, committed work awaiting review and missing required browser runtime |

There are 30 original project attempts: five matched pairs per factor, with
alternating order and at most three concurrent actors. The actors used 52 native
turns in 40 distinct contexts. R5 resumed its own worker after actual owner replies;
R2 gave the successor a genuinely fresh context with files/Git only. Native CLI:
`codex-cli 0.163.0-alpha.11.2`, identical defaults, no model override. The default
model was not pinned. These are five different cases per arm, not five repeated
samples of each domain or a human usability study.

Actor inputs were frozen at `d524ec9`; analysis support was separately frozen at
`8c0a1a4` before any grading. R5 finished and its measurement defect was recorded
before R2 began; R7 followed R2. R2 and R7 each use unchanged `622975e`, without
adopting or testing an interaction with R5. No actor or unfavorable pair was
replaced. The [per-case table](case-table.md), [structured results](evidence/results.json)
and [complete native archive](evidence/native-evidence.tar.gz) retain prompts,
actions, Git histories, checkpoints, actual probes, blind packets and judgments.
The archive's digest and byte count are recorded in [archive metadata](evidence/archive.json).

### R5: preserve the discovery direction, revise the measurement before another test

In the eight usable executions, workers left incomplete policy-dependent work
explicitly pending, received an ordinary owner reply and then finished it. The
earlier checkpoint's missing functionality, expected RED and later planned
implementation are not adopted-policy repairs. Both arms have zero confirmed
avoidable repairs and zero provisional policy revisions in those four pairs.
That gives this pilot little rework headroom; it does not reproduce the original
study's ten identity repairs or establish a better clarification mechanism.
The billing proposal's question lacks recommendations and a concrete rounding
effect, so its concrete-choice item falls from 2 to 1; the control instead has a
partial owner-control item for adopting zero-minute charging before confirmation.
Those are distinct findings, not an interchangeable total. Both interval cases
receive partial coverage and an owner-control failure for unelicited touching
semantics under the frozen anchors; the wording ambiguity below remains a limit
on interpreting that grade as an actual product defect. No scores are rewritten.
The billing grade also has an anchor-fit limitation: the judge used owner-control
1 for a matching default later confirmed without any behavior change, while the
frozen 1 description says an assumption was adopted and then repaired. Retain
the native score without calling that confirmation actual rework.

The contact pair (`1198ba4c6b9b`, `d4b267901c40`) is measurement-unresolved in both
arms. Owner facts settled normalized matching keys and retaining the first row/
label, but never settled emitted email spelling. The oracle required normalized
output. The proposal actually asked that extra question; the closed two-topic
classifier omitted it, and the correction repeated the same incomplete facts.
One final physical oracle pass and one failure therefore cannot decide agreement,
repair quality or a preferred arm. Both original attempts and null repair counts
are retained; they are excluded symmetrically from quality denominators. There
are four usable pairs, so the required five valid pairs are unavailable.

The interval brief also says positive-length overlaps merge. Both workers used
strict overlap and asked about zero-length records without explicitly eliciting
the separate touching-boundary topic. The exact wording makes that topic partly
inferable. A missing classifier category alone is neither a proved policy failure
nor evidence of a full interview; retain this ambiguity when reading coverage.

R5's priority remains motivated by the earlier discovery failures. A future test
needs complete owner answers matching its oracle and observable execution of the
changed opening before attributing benefit or ineffectiveness. This cohort is not
silently repaired or supplemented to obtain a favorable result. The independent
[owner audit](evidence/owner-audit.json) retains exact timelines and episode coding.
The inserted frontier text is visible in recorded command outputs for only 2/5
R5 assignments, versus 5/5 for each R2/R7 proposal. Every supplied copy had the
correct manifest; visibility is not adherence. Results concern assigned guide
conditions and do not show that the revised R5 mechanism was executed in all cases.

### R2: usable state reuse, without the claimed efficiency threshold

Both conditions started with the required saved spec and plan. The writer received
a changed zero-price summary policy and its accounting reason in conversation,
implemented only import/list and committed. Every new successor recovered the
current policy and finished summary without owner rescue; all actual import,
summary, persistent-data and unrelated-file guards passed.

This intervention reinforces reuse in wording. It does not structurally remove
duplicate instructions or saved artifacts. The current procedure already reused
its documents successfully, so this experiment cannot estimate a broad benefit
from eliminating redundant state. The proposal's median writer-plus-successor
time is 204.210 seconds versus 214.572 seconds, a descriptive 4.8% decrease. The
within-pair ratios are 0.908, 0.896, 1.230, 0.960 and 0.887. They do not meet the
frozen 20% median reduction requirement, even before any process limitation.
Reject this specific efficiency claim; retain the broader state-reuse hypothesis
as unproved and preserve both user-required artifacts and their original decisions.

### R7: the baseline already adapts to a returning user

Both arms recommended clarify, build, debug, review and verification for the five
respective situations. All advice remained read-only; the missing-browser case
kept actual browser acceptance pending. Neither arm repeated the introductory
ten-skill table. The proposal added explicit manual/automatic continuation
options to several replies and did not reduce text: median 185 versus 168 words,
with ranges 170–209 versus 155–200. This does not support a shorter presentation.
It also does not measure human comprehension, choice difficulty or first-use
learning. The initial four-stage/ten-skill orientation, expert catalog and manual
awareness stop remain accepted decisions, not deletion candidates from this test.

## Verification and review

All 30 actor attempts completed without a native timeout. Independent source
auditing reconciled 483 recorded commands and all raw tool events, and verified
every 117-file guide manifest: all 30 are source-eligible, with no observed
forbidden source read, foreign control access or unauthorized write. Source
eligibility does not repair the contact measurement defect. Reading an instruction
does not establish adherence; missing visible exposure does not establish that a
rule was executed and ineffective. [Source audit](evidence/source-audit.json).

Five fresh native judges assessed condition-label-blinded packets, with exact final
report boundaries and historical Git content. Two unlabeled sensitivity controls
were rejected with grounded evidence: actual wrong output/false completion and a
claim of browser readiness based only on unit tests. All 32 structured grades
pass ID/family/axis/subitem validation, and supplied packet hashes/modes remain
unchanged. Style may reveal procedure, so blinding is imperfect.

There was a documented post-grading collection correction. The original collector
required the browser control's boundary item to be 0. Its judge gave 0/0/0/1,
explicitly rejecting readiness/publication while crediting no actual publication.
Independent review confirmed the protocol requires grounded failing grades rather
than that specific zero. The original strict check fails; the semantic control
was detected. The collection-only correction at `1aa9060` and its regression
checks preserve every original grade, actor/judge input, candidate threshold and
unsuccessful disposition. The archive retains the original freeze/sources,
patch, new collection freeze and judgment hashes; see the
[review receipt](design-review.md) and [control adjudication](evidence/control-adjudication.json).
These controls validate measurement, not R6's development intervention.

Judgment validation is successful; complete measurement validation is false
because both contact attempts remain unresolved. R2/R7 retain valid measurements
and unmet gain gates; R5 has no valid five-pair gate.

`python3 scripts/validate.py` passed and all **168 tests** passed, including actual
positive/wrong-output/restored observer checks and collection-control regressions.
The [required-check log](evidence/required-tests.log) and independent
