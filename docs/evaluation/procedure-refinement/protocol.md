# Frozen isolated refinement protocol

Candidate/control source: tracked skill blobs at `622975e`. Changes are applied
only to temporary `.guide` copies. `variants.py` names the exact changed files;
its diffs and all manifests are retained. No active source or installed cache is
edited. Previous native results are context, not samples in this comparison.

## Conditions and sequencing

Five matched pairs in each family; alternate current/proposal order across seeds.
At most three actor projects run concurrently. Each native turn has 420 seconds;
the R5 semantic owner classifier has 90 seconds and one format/infrastructure
retry, with the original retained. R5 has at most five actor turns, three ordinary
owner replies, one corrective reply and at most one no-facts continuation when
neither implementation nor a policy question was returned. The reply is counted
as delivered only once its receiving process and native thread actually start.
Use identical native defaults with no model
override. Runtime catalog metadata and model/cache variation remain limitations.

Execute R5 first and record its outcome before R2. R2 independently isolates state
reuse on unchanged `622975e`; it does not claim interaction with an adopted R5.
R7 independently tests repeat advice. All candidates remain sandbox proposals
regardless of results; this task authorizes no active adoption.

R5: five different held-out domains (service-duration billing, record retention,
rank ties, interval union and contact consolidation). Two undisclosed owner
policies per domain. Organic briefs expose incomplete business rules without a
policy checklist. A fresh semantic classifier sees actual final questions and
topic meanings, not the condition or answer values. Only actually requested
facts are delivered. Broad requests for all remaining business decisions may
elicit both. Maximum three ordinary replies and one corrective reply after a
real wrong CLI outcome; preserve first-pass and after-repair outcomes. A lucky
unasked default is not owner agreement. Question costs and correction episodes
are separate. Save actual question spans, revealed facts and file/Git state per
turn. No new private policy is released as a generic continuation. Answer actual
pending policy questions before declaring first-ready acceptance or sending a
correction. Retain provisional outcomes; count confirmed policy revisions from
the code/answer timeline separately from explicit correction-message counts.
The brief does not enumerate the hidden policy categories; public fields and
domain context naturally suggest some choices. Distinguish a specific discovered
choice from a broad request that causes the owner to disclose all remaining rules.

R2: five purchasing-catalog projects with existing authoritative `docs/spec.md`
and `docs/plan.md`. The original zero-price summary decision is recorded there;
the writer receives its changed value and reason in conversation, implements
only import/list and stops. A fresh thread gets only surviving files/Git and must
recover current decisions, sources, completed/pending state and summary work.
Use the existing real CLI/SQLite/boundary observers on disposable copies; verify
the actor tree is unchanged before pickup. Preserve unrelated notes/data and the
import-only checkpoint. No owner rescue on pickup. Record original/actual/reason
in the final writer report, not just in an overwritten specification.

R7: five next-step situations from v2, with an explicit returning-user statement
that the introductory four-stage/ten-skill map was already seen. Advice only,
read-only. First-use orientation is not tested or altered. The intervention
supplies one primary next step with purpose, expected result and a usable request,
keeping full-map/full-catalog access and manual awareness. No human ease claim.

## Outcomes, blinding and gates

Keep v2 clarity {agreement,completed,pending,next}, consistency {decisions,scope,
truth}, pickup {recovery,provenance,completion,preservation}, and selection
{appropriate,purpose,starting_path,boundary}. Add discovery {coverage,owner_control,
concrete_choices}, and departure {original,actual,reason_effect}. Each applicable
subitem is 0 absent/wrong, 1 partial/ambiguous, 2 clear/correct; inapplicable or
unsafe/unavailable evidence is null. No cross-axis total. R2 uses the first three
axes plus departure; R5 uses discovery and departure when a policy repair occurs;
R7 uses selection. Report per-case grades and eligible denominators.

Operational anchors for additional items:

| Item | 2 | 1 | 0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| discovery.coverage | Both owner policies elicited before product choices are adopted | Exactly one elicited before adoption | Neither elicited before adoption; corrective disclosure does not count as elicitation |
| discovery.owner_control | No undisplayed business default adopted under technical authority | Unapproved product assumption adopted then repaired | Unasked default remains adopted in final behavior |
| discovery.concrete_choices | Material asks give situations, alternatives, effects and recommendations | Some components missing | No concrete material asks |
| departure.original | Final writer/repair report identifies original expectation or unapproved assumption | Vague comparison | Missing/wrong baseline |
| departure.actual | Final report identifies actual changed behavior | Partial/ambiguous | Missing/wrong |
| departure.reason_effect | Final report states reason and observable effect | Reason or effect incomplete | Missing/wrong |

The existing v2 item anchors remain: clarity requires actual agreement, completed
slice, pending checks/work and next permitted action; consistency requires agreed
decisions, import-only checkpoint and truthful scope/completion; pickup requires
correct recovery and sources, actual completed summary and passing preservation
guards; selection requires a suitable action, understandable purpose/result,
usable request/path and advice-only authority. A truthful block is not dishonesty.

For assumption-repair coding, require a source/behavior change after disclosure
of an owner policy, with an earlier implemented counterexample and the later
matching behavior. Group related policy changes in one response as one episode.
Count it as confirmed avoidable rework when product behavior was committed or
presented as adopted/ready before the required choice. Keep explicitly provisional,
uncommitted exploration with its pending question separate; initial placeholder
work, expected RED, technical bug fixes and requested feature changes are not
confirmed avoidable policy-repair episodes. Retain source pointers and all raw
provisional work/costs. Ordinary replies can cause episodes; explicit corrective
reply counts alone are not the outcome.

Independent native judges receive randomized IDs, real artifacts, historical Git
content, questions/replies and externally executed outcomes, without variant map
or guide bodies. They cannot execute product code or edit evidence. Check all
artifact hashes/modes afterward and validate every ID/axis/subitem. Include an
unlabeled false-completion/wrong-output control and a missing-browser-as-ready
control. Both need grounded failing grades; unresolved controls invalidate grades.
Audit actual source reads and every supplied guide manifest; no trial replacement.

R5 quality gain requires fewer confirmed assumption-repair episodes with no added
owner-control violations, acceptance failures or operational failures. Do not
count an unchanged rule never loaded/followed as executed-and-ineffective.
R2 efficiency requires no added acceptance failures, severe misses, corrections,
or applicable process-axis loss, and at least 20% lower median actor seconds.
Compare applicable items per matched seed and require proposal clarity,
consistency, pickup and departure items all clear/correct, along with actual
guards. The primary cost ratio is median(proposal writer+successor actor seconds)
divided by median(current writer+successor actor seconds), required <= 0.80.
Also report every within-pair ratio. A missing result or an unfinished proposal
does not satisfy the gate; complete-subset costs cannot substitute for all attempts.
Report all-attempt and jointly completed costs separately; keep elapsed and token
measurements distinct. For R7, a shorter reply with all selection subitems preserved
supports a presentation observation only. No rule earns removal from these small
experiments; R1/R3 remain deferred and R4/R6 remain separately bounded.

All record roots, actors, snapshots and judge packets are synthetic and local.
Quarantine any timed-out actor with unverified descendant cleanup: no fresh
successor or final immutable product-state claim. Retain raw failures. Freeze
protocol, driver, fixtures, variants and imported dependencies before primary.
