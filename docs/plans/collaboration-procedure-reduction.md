Thus no procedure is established as ineffective or automatically removable.
The comparison supports a targeted redesign with explicit preservation of the
observed control/rework benefits. It does not show that their value covers the
cost. One timed-out COCO change and known owner-classifier errors remain in the
report; they are not silently repaired or converted to valid completions.

## Constraints that remain

The user's accepted decisions in [staged development](../specs/staged-development.md)
remain authoritative: material product decisions belong to the user; manual
skill awareness is the default; explicit automatic basic execution is scoped to
one spec and stops before push; appropriate real verification and work-unit
commits remain required. Saved spec and plan are explicit user choices, not
an upstream necessity. Reducing their duplication is different from waiving them.
Any proposal to remove those obligations needs a concrete separate user decision.

The current comparison measures an explicitly automatic temporary-project
profile, not first-use learning, all specialists, publication, human time, abrupt
crashes, or the three original trees. Do not generalize its cost screen to every
skill or remove mechanisms solely because this benchmark already has a ceiling.

## Recorded experiment designs

**R5: repair discovery, then measure its price.** In a dedicated feature branch,
replace the opening's vague invitation with a short, concrete account of what
is agreed, which behavior-changing choices remain, and which reversible details
the agent can choose. Put that frontier in the existing spec/plan; add no third
tracker or mandatory extra round. For an import task, distinguish key identity
(whether ` A1 ` and `a1` denote one item) from duplicate resolution, malformed
rows and the later summary policy. Ask current-slice material choices before
writing product code; defer later-slice choices explicitly. Technical automation
permission does not adopt an undisplayed business default.

This is motivated by all ten identity repairs, baseline seed 4's early collision
implementation, and COCO seed 0's loaded-but-unfollowed clarification preview.
It is not evidence that every task needs those four particular questions or a
longer checklist. Test five new fixed matched projects with materially different
ambiguous domains, under current COCO versus R5 only. Freeze hidden owner facts,
negative controls and outcome rubric before execution; retain all runs. Require
fewer confirmed assumption-repair episodes without new owner-control violations,
acceptance failures, severe review misses or operational failures. Report extra
questions, elapsed/token cost and any deferred choices; do not trade an owner
decision for a lucky compatible default. If discovery does not improve, do not
ship an expanded interview merely because it sounds careful.

The other chat's `5164cd4` adds useful future preservation cases: recover an
already-recorded material choice without re-asking, and surface a truly unknown
choice that can safely remain pending. Score truth/ownership separately from
no-intervention completion. A safe question is not lost context or dishonesty;
a lucky matching default and fewer questions cannot replace agreement. Preserve
this distinction when repairing the owner/oracle mismatch and activation path
identified by the completed pilot; do not merely replay the failed opening change.

**R2: explore state reuse while preserving the two saved artifacts.** Once R5
has a recorded decision, branch separately to compare the accepted baseline
with one change: update the existing feature spec and sole execution plan at
scope changes/resume, link prior decisions, and avoid additional overlapping
trackers or repeated contract blocks. A material new feature may still need its
own spec; this experiment concerns continuation of the same feature. First audit
whether each trace actually duplicates state. COCO seed 2 already reuses its
documents yet times out, so extra files cannot be asserted as that timeout's cause.

Preserve the initial rework and owner-control behavior, new-thread recovery,
original-versus-actual reason reporting, real verification and commits. Use five
fresh matched pairs and the removal acceptance gate below: no added acceptance
failures, severe misses or corrections and at least 20% lower median actor cost.
Also retain policy-rework counts, unasked material choices and completion rates.
Report all-attempt and jointly completed costs separately. If a comparable cost
gain is absent, reject the efficiency claim. This is exploratory redesign after
a mixed screen, not a claim that R2 was causally identified here.

Freeze applicable v2 rubric items as per-matched-case preservation guards before
R2 runs. Require clear agreement/completed/pending/next action; consistent
decisions, checkpoint and truthful completion; correct recovered choices and usable
sources; actual summary/import/boundary success. Also require the final writer
report to distinguish original expectation, actual changed choice and its reason/
effect. Updating the spec does not reset the original comparison baseline. These
are existing accepted obligations; record missed items without creating another
mutable tracker. A cheaper CLI-correct result that weakens them does not meet the
reduction gate.

Preserve an explicitly open material choice in the surviving record as well as
settled facts. A fresh worker with the same explicit technical authority must
recover usable sources, completed/pending work, evidence, next action and scope,
finish permitted work and preserve real behavior. It must not erase an unknown
choice to manufacture completion. Renewed technical authority does not prove
cross-chat consent persistence; model pickup does not establish human ease.

**R7: compare repeat advice separately.** V2 observed actionable recommendations
in all five current cases and ten-skill overview tables in four. Test five matched
returning-user situations, with the prior map exposure explicit in both arms.
Preserve suitable action, purpose/expected result, usable starting request and
advice-only authority, with no per-case rubric loss. Keep first-use orientation
unchanged. Measure final reply words/procedure references descriptively; actual
human understanding, satisfaction and learning require their own participants.
This is a presentation hypothesis, separate from deferred R1 review-body loading.

### R7 human usability remains a separate conditional experiment

`5164cd4` proposes actual participants after identical first orientation, whereas
the completed pilot generated model advice. Neither substitutes for the other.
Before scheduling a participant pilot, identify actual user friction and a real
presentation difference: the later current model arm already omitted the repeated
table in all five cases. Do not fabricate a verbose current arm. If no distinct
problem/treatment exists, retain the baseline and defer R7.

For an eligible pilot, keep the four-stage/ten-skill first orientation identical;
vary only repeated full-map display on follow-up recommendations. Preserve the
same next skill, reason, purpose/result, copyable request, manual selection,
full-list/expert access and relevant specialist discovery. Explicit requests
remain selected; automatic flow is spec-scoped and ends before push; specialist
opt-ins remain separate. Consent and runtime boundaries are not shortened.

Use at least five actual participants unfamiliar with COCO, counterbalanced
equivalent follow-up tasks and presentation order. Freeze assignment, scoring
and the primary human outcome/threshold before observation. Observe correct
next-action choice, explanation, specialist/list discovery and approval
understanding; record participant active time, re-explanations and mistakes
separately from response length or actor costs. Accept only with no observed
loss of required comprehension, discovery, user control or verification boundaries.
Small-sample observations do not establish population learning effects.

Without participants, human outcomes stay unavailable. This does not block
unrelated correctness/authority work or justify extra model rounds. R7 supplies
no R1 removal nomination or permission to delete initial orientation. Its human
outcome is unmeasured, rather than disproved by the longer model replies.

R4 requires state-specific evidence; both conditions repeat checks and retry Git
permissions. Do not delete meaningful checks or classify operational retries as
ceremony. R6 needs separate held-out tasks on which independent reviewers or a
deliberately executed test mutation could reveal a missed fault. Do not combine
both changes or keep choosing easier-to-win tasks after seeing results.

## Execution and acceptance

1. Close the current report, map each nomination to an actual trace and owner,
   and state the favorable, mixed or null family result. No rule-specific causal
   attribution follows from the two-arm experiment alone.
2. For the first eligible candidate, make a dedicated branch and saved small
   spec/plan with one factor changed. Preserve provenance and decision rationale.
3. Run five fresh matched pairs, alternating order and keeping task facts,
   capabilities, runtime settings, budgets and blind outcome grading equal.
   Include negative sensitivity controls and initial failures. Do not run a large
   combined rewrite whose successful outcome cannot identify the useful change.
4. For a removal, require no new acceptance failures, severe defect misses or
   owner corrections, no loss of applicable demonstrated process deliverables,
   and at least 20% lower median actor cost under the frozen
   operating gate. Report elapsed and token costs separately. Five pairs are a
   bounded operational decision, not statistical proof of equivalence.
5. If quality or owner control degrades, retain the obligation and redesign its
   implementation. If gain is absent, do not call the new procedure better.
   Commit the result and its unfavorable evidence by work unit.
6. Ask the user only for a concrete change to an accepted product/workflow choice,
   explaining the proposed behavior, measured tradeoff and retained safeguards.
   Publication and global installation remain separate requests.
