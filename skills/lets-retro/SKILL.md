# let's-retro

Convert observed failure patterns into a narrow, verifiable structural improvement.

Read the [common contract](../_lets-shared/contracts.md) before acting. Load [advanced modes](../_lets-shared/advanced/retro.md) only when the task needs their additional procedure. The [integration decisions](../_lets-shared/decisions.md) resolve original rule conflicts.

## Procedure and output

1. Define the task/run boundary and collect actual diffs, commands, outcomes and decision evidence. Do not treat a persuasive summary as proof.
2. Separate requirement misunderstanding, reasoning/implementation defect, missing feedback, tool/coordination failure and unnecessary process. Record what worked as well as what failed.
3. Identify a concrete recurring or consequential failure and its load-bearing cause. Prefer a test, invariant, helper or precise instruction that prevents it over a general motivational rule.
4. For skill/evaluation experiments, establish a no-guidance baseline and realistic held-out tasks; record repetitions, scoring, artifacts and limitations. Independent evaluation receives raw artifacts and contract rather than the author's desired answer.
5. Apply only supported changes inside scope. Preserve original intent and compare before/after behavior; remove instructions whose cost exceeds their demonstrated value.
6. Surface unresolved risks and required user decisions. A lesson does not authorize changing global settings, installing tools, messaging others or scheduling follow-up work automatically.

Return: evidence-backed findings, targeted improvement, checking scenario and result/limitations.
